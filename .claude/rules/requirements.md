# Requirements

- Each requirement follows the template of the lecture and has one ID. Never change or reuse an ID, and never give an
  ID a suffix such as `b`.
- No requirement ever had the ID FR-1 or FR-2, and the requirement FR-7 no longer exists. Do not use these three IDs.
  The next new requirement is FR-20.
- Write the Statement and the sentences below it that bind Coffee Kitty with the key words of RFC 2119: MUST and MUST
  NOT for a rule, SHOULD for a plausibility check, and MAY for an optional input.
- List the requirements that a requirement depends on under Supporting Information, and list the requirement in each of
  them in return.
- Give every number in a requirement a source in the same file: a quoted input of a stakeholder, a technical limit with
  its reason, or an open TBD. An open TBD names a contact person and states what the requirement specifies until that
  person answers.
- Do not add a limit without one of these sources, and do not copy a limit from the coffee app of SE@UHD without asking
  the stakeholder.
